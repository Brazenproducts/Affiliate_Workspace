Bartact Invented the Paracord Grab Handle — and Built Them for the Bronco

Before Bartact, there were no paracord grab handles for off-road vehicles. Bartact built the first ones for Jeep Wrangler: genuine US military-specification Type III 550 paracord wrapped around a solid steel core formed to each vehicle's specific roll bar tube diameter. The concept worked and the copies followed — brands that saw the market and built lighter-spec alternatives using decorative paracord and foam cores to hit lower price points.

When Ford launched the sixth-generation Bronco in 2021, Bartact extended the same original concept to the Bronco. Not by adapting Jeep handles to fit the Bronco's different roll bar geometry. By engineering a Bronco-specific steel core formed to the Bronco's roll bar tube dimensions and building Bronco-specific handles from that core. The result is a grab handle that fits the Bronco's roll bar with the same precision that Bartact's Jeep handles fit Jeep roll bars — because it was built for the Bronco specifically, not adapted from something else.

This matters on the Bronco the same way it matters on any purpose-built off-road vehicle. The Bronco is designed for serious trail use — it has a proper frame, solid front and rear axles on Badlands and Rubicon-equivalent trims, and enough capability to put passengers in situations where they need to brace hard. A grab handle with a foam core and decorative cord isn't appropriate for that use. Bartact's Bronco handles are.

Type III 550 Paracord — The Military Specification

Bartact uses genuine Type III 550 paracord in every Bronco grab handle in every color option. Type III is the US military classification for 7-strand inner core paracord with a minimum 550 lb tensile strength rating. This is the cord specification used in US military parachute suspension systems. Not the decorative paracord available by the yard at hobby stores. Not lightweight utility cord sold under the paracord name without the Type III construction. The genuine military-specification cord that defines what paracord is.

The key properties that matter in a grab handle application are tensile strength for passenger safety under trail loading, UV resistance for topless Bronco operation in summer sun, abrasion resistance for repeated gripping with gloved and wet hands, and temperature stability across the full range of conditions the Bronco is designed to operate in — from sand dunes in summer heat to mountain trails in winter cold. Type III 550 is specified for all of these because it was designed for a real-world safety application, not a decorative one. That specification is why Bartact uses it and why the copies that use lighter cord produce inferior handles.

Solid Steel Core — Built for Bronco Roll Bar Geometry

The Bronco's roll bar uses specific tube dimensions that differ from Jeep's JL and JK roll bar geometries. Bartact builds a Bronco-specific steel core formed to the Bronco's actual tube dimensions. Not a Jeep core adapted for the Bronco. Not a universal core sized to fit both. A Bronco-specific form that matches the Bronco's roll bar with the same precision that Bartact's Jeep cores match Jeep roll bars.

The solid steel core is what prevents the handle from developing play over time. The failure mechanism of foam and plastic cores is wet-dry cycling — moisture gets in, dries, the foam compresses slightly, the handle develops a small amount of play on the mounting surface. Over months of trail use with repeated wet exposure, a foam-core handle becomes noticeably loose. A solid steel core doesn't compress. The dimensional fit between the steel core and the roll bar tube is the same on day one as it is three years later. Zero play at install, zero play on year three.

Bolt-On Installation — No Drilling, 10 Minutes

Bartact Bronco grab handles use the factory-threaded mounting positions on the Bronco's roll bar. No drilling into your Bronco's roll bar. No modification to the vehicle. All mounting hardware is included — stainless steel throughout.

Installation: identify the front roll bar mounting positions — two per side, driver and passenger, on the A-pillar roll bar section. On the 4-door Bronco, identify the rear sport bar positions behind the rear seats for rear handle installation. Thread the included stainless hardware through the handle loop and into the factory mounting hole. Snug with a standard socket — moderate hand-tight into the Bronco's aluminum mounting boss. Check for zero side-to-side play on each installed handle. Total installation time for a full set: approximately 10 minutes.

No additional tools required beyond a standard socket set. No adhesives. No modifications that need to be reversed if you later remove the handles. The installation is fully reversible — the handles can be removed, swapped for different colors, or transferred to a compatible vehicle.
