Type III 550 Paracord — What "550" Actually Means

The name "550 paracord" refers to a minimum tensile strength of 550 lbs. Type III is the US military classification that specifies a 7-strand inner core construction with that minimum tensile rating. This is the cord specification used in US military parachutes — not as a marketing claim, but as the literal application from which the specification gets its name. It's UV-resistant, abrasion-resistant, and temperature-stable across the range of conditions a Jeep operates in: Arizona desert summer heat, Colorado mountain winters, trail use, highway driving.

Bartact sources genuine Type III 550 paracord for every grab handle in every color option. There are no economy colors built from lighter cord. Every color — all 30+ of them — uses the same cord to the same specification. Only the dye changes. This consistency is part of why Bartact grabs hold up identically regardless of color choice.

Solid Steel Core — Custom-Formed for JL Roll Bar Geometry

The JL uses a rectangular-profile roll bar tube. The core of a Bartact JL grab handle is solid steel formed to that specific rectangular profile. Not formed to an approximation, not formed to a round profile that fits inside a rectangular tube, not formed to a universal profile that fits several vehicles poorly. Formed specifically to the JL rectangular roll bar's tube geometry.

This matters because core fit determines whether the handle stays locked to the roll bar over time. A core that doesn't match the tube geometry precisely will rock slightly on the mounting surface. That rocking compresses and deforms whatever the core is made of with each load cycle — and on a foam or plastic core, that deformation is permanent. Over months of use, a foam-core handle on a round-tube application develops audible play. Bartact's solid steel core on the correct tube profile doesn't develop play — the contact surface doesn't compress, and the geometry doesn't allow rotation.

Choosing the Right Configuration for Your JL Grab Handles

The JL Wrangler is available in 2-door (JL) and 4-door Unlimited (JLU) configurations, and both have specific grab handle installation positions that Bartact covers.

On the 2-door JL, the primary installation positions are the front roll bar — two positions per side, driver and passenger. The 2-door JL does not have a sport bar behind a rear row of seats in the same configuration as the JLU, so rear handle positioning differs from the 4-door setup.

On the 4-door JLU, the front roll bar positions are the same as the 2-door. The JLU additionally has sport bar positions behind the rear seat row — equivalent to the rear grab bar positions on the JK JKU. Bartact builds handles for all of these positions. Ordering a full set for the JLU includes front and rear handle configurations appropriate to the 4-door's interior layout.
